# Data attribution and licences

The dashboard normalizes and reformats public source data. Each normalized poll retains its original source URL where supplied, attribution, and licence metadata. Current county vote-count rows retain their source URL, source name, reporting time, and retrieval time, including explicit attribution when counts come through a secondary mirror. The application displays those records and links; this document does not replace row-specific obligations.

| Material | Attribution and terms |
|---|---|
| Forecasts, polling summaries and pollster ratings | [Polling Forecast](https://www.pollingforecast.com/us/data), generally [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Their model probabilities, modeled margins and reported polling averages are distinct metrics. |
| Individual primary-provider polls | Original pollster and release URL, distributed through Polling Forecast. Consult each row’s `licence` and attribution. Rows identified as figures from Wikipedia credit Wikipedia contributors and are subject to [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Adapted normalized rows derived from those figures are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Other poll figures retain their source-specific terms. |
| Supplementary polls | [VoteHub](https://votehub.com/polls/api/) and original pollsters, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). |
| Current 2026 county returns | Official public election feeds from [Georgia](https://results.sos.ga.gov/) (159 counties, Senate and governor), [North Carolina](https://er.ncsbe.gov/) (100 counties, Senate), [California](https://media.sos.ca.gov/) (58 counties, governor; awaiting reports in the verified observation), [Arizona](https://app.enhancedvoting.com/results/public/Arizona/elections/110326GeneralElection) (15 counties, governor ticket), [Virginia](https://enr.elections.virginia.gov/results/public/virginia/elections/2026-November-General) (133 counties/independent cities, Senate), [Ohio](https://liveresults.ohiosos.gov/) (88 counties, Senate and governor), [Colorado](https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/CO/127407/web.345435/) (64 counties, Senate and governor), and [Minnesota](https://electionresultsfiles.sos.mn.gov/20261103/allracesbycounty.txt) (87 counties, Senate and governor). Source names, direct feed links, and source timestamps remain attached to each report. Counts are unofficial until certified; initialized zero-vote rows are awaiting reports. No blanket Creative Commons licence is asserted for state data. No historical county election-return dataset is published. |
| Race roster and incumbents | [Ballotpedia Senate roster](https://ballotpedia.org/United_States_Senate_elections,_2026), [Ballotpedia governor roster](https://ballotpedia.org/Gubernatorial_elections,_2026), [U.S. Senate Class II roster](https://www.senate.gov/senators/Class_II.htm), and Polling Forecast race pages. Provider candidate fields may omit other ballot candidates. |
| Kalshi quotes and contract terms | [Kalshi public market-data API](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/quick_start_market_data). Exact market identifiers, contract wording and source links are retained for verification. No Creative Commons licence is asserted for market data or contract text. |
| Polymarket quotes and resolution terms | [Polymarket public market-data APIs](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/overview). Exact event/market identifiers, outcome metadata, resolution information and event URLs are retained. No Creative Commons licence is asserted for market data or resolution text. |
| Map boundaries | [us-atlas v3](https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas), derived from U.S. Census Bureau boundaries. The packaged us-atlas data is [ISC licensed](https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas/blob/master/LICENSE). Local GeoJSON is converted from the original TopoJSON for rendering. These are geographic boundaries, not a claim about election administration boundaries. |
| Interface icons | [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/license), ISC licence. Installed dependencies retain their respective licences. |

Recorded fixtures and saved snapshots retain the same source-specific licensing as the source records. The project does not replace source attribution with a blanket licence over all data. The source data is presented for information; no trading, wallet, signing, or account functionality is implemented.

Reported 2026 counts: official state election offices via [Open America](https://openamerica.io/elections/api/), with feed timestamps and direct data links. These unofficial counts are not race calls or certification. The exit-poll view includes one dated factual reading attributed to [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/cbs-news-exit-polls-midterm-elections-2026/); it is labeled as a recorded observation and is not republished as a live data feed.

Reported leader cards and map colors derive only from actual reported candidate vote totals. Market quotes are displayed for the same matched candidate and never establish a leader or a winner. Where county reports are aggregated, only distinct counties from the same complete source and office are summed; no partial-provider estimate, model probability, or market value enters the vote total. Arizona requests respect a two-minute minimum interval, Ohio a three-minute interval, and Texas requests align to :00 and :30. Other connected county feeds use a one-minute interval, with Minnesota's shared collector updating about every 15 minutes.

Texas county returns: Texas Secretary of State via [Texian Partisan's public election-results mirror](https://texianpartisan.com/election-night-2026/). This is an explicitly attributed secondary mirror, not a direct official connection. Only factual vote counts and reporting metadata are used; editorial material and race calls are not imported. Original source and mirror collection times are preserved. Counts were cross-checked against the [Harris County official results JSON](https://appfiles.harrisvotes.com/harrisvotes/prd/Data/1126/resultsbyballot.json) and a separate public county-results mirror. No blanket Creative Commons licence is asserted for this feed.

Primary nationwide 2026 vote data: [The New York Times public election results](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/11/03/us/elections/results-senate.html), with AP decision authority only where the published record explicitly identifies `ap`. The dashboard imports factual candidate identities, party labels, reported vote counts, reporting units, source times, and call records; it excludes images, editorial prose and final-outcome model forecasts. Source counting-progress estimates remain distinct from actual counted votes. No blanket Creative Commons or republication licence is asserted. County source timestamps are race-feed timestamps when the publisher supplies no per-county clock. Alaska uses 40 House election districts; [Census 2024 Alaska lower-chamber boundaries](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/cb_2024_02_sldl_500k.zip) are public government geographic data and are not mislabeled as county boundaries. New England town totals are summed only using county FIPS provided in the source, without invented allocations.

The Congress tab imports explicit AP-attributed calls and publisher-designated seat gains from the same NYT-hosted public result files. Continuing Senate party-group baselines are labeled Democratic/aligned where the source includes caucusing independents. House seats are validated against the 2020 apportionment used for the 2026 election. Estimated vote-in percentages are separately attributed source estimates; town rollups use source expected-vote weights, not precinct ratios or a proprietary election forecast.
